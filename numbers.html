What Folsom estate houses sell for

How this was counted: a sale is in the estate group if the listing agent checked Trust, Probate Listing or Subject to Court Confirmation on the MetroList listing. A sale with more than one of those boxes counts once. Every other Folsom single-family sale is the comparison group. Same 12 months, same 762 sales. The group mixes sale types, a trust sale is not always an inheritance, and the comparison does not adjust for condition, size or location. Read it as a description of what sold, not proof of cause.
