What is my inherited house in Folsom worth?

By Scott Kirk, Broker/Owner · Updated November 4, 2026

Short answer

Folsom's median sale over the last 12 months was $765,000. In the seven north-side neighborhoods below, medians ran from $742,500 in Willow Creek to $1,525,000 in Hillcrest. An inherited home often needs updating, so it may sell below its neighborhood median. And an estate has two values to understand, the date of death value for taxes and today's value for the sale, plus an extra appraisal requirement if the sale needs court confirmation.

Folsom neighborhood medians

Two values, plus one more for a court sale

Value 1 The date of death value For: the tax basis. See taxes. Set by: the probate referee in a probate, or a licensed appraiser outside probate.
Value 2 Today's value, as is For: the list price and the family's split. Set by: recent sales near the house, in similar condition.
If a court confirms the sale The confirmation appraisal For: a court confirmed sale, where the price must be at least 90 percent of it. Set by: an appraisal dated within one year before the confirmation hearing.

Source: Probate Code 8902 and Probate Code 10309.

Do trust and probate houses sell for less in Folsom?

Not per square foot, in this year's sales. From November 1, 2025 to October 31, 2026, 82 of Folsom's 762 single family sales were marked trust, probate or court confirmation in MetroList. The two groups had similar median prices per square foot: $384 against $382. This group's median days on market was 12, against 17. That counts days listed, not the time it takes to get authority to sell.

The median sale price was lower, $727,475 against $775,000. These houses were also a little smaller and older: a median of 1,990 square feet against 2,097, and built in 1995 against 2000. The group mixes trust sales, probate listings and court confirmation sales, and a trust sale is not always an inheritance. Price the house for the condition it is in today, not for the neighborhood median.

How do I price a house nobody has updated?

Compare two groups of sales near the house: homes that sold in original condition and homes that sold updated. The gap shows what buyers paid for updates. Before I recommend any work, I compare the likely sale proceeds against the contractor bids, the carrying costs the estate pays while the work is done, like the tax bill, insurance and utilities, and the time it adds.
