What is my inherited house in Folsom worth?

By Scott Kirk, Broker/Owner · Updated November 4, 2026

Short answer

Folsom's median sale over the last 12 months was $765,000. In the seven north-side neighborhoods below, medians ran from $742,500 in Willow Creek to $1,525,000 in Hillcrest. An inherited home often needs updating, so it may sell below its neighborhood median. And an estate has two values to understand, the date of death value for taxes and today's value for the sale, plus an extra appraisal requirement if the sale needs court confirmation. Median sale price for every Folsom neighborhood with at least 5 sales in the last 12 months, drawn on the county's recorded subdivision maps. A median is a starting point, not an appraisal. The date of death value and the listing price both come from the sales closest to the house, in size, age and condition.

Two values, plus one more for a court sale

Value 1 The date of death value For: the tax basis. See taxes. Set by: the probate referee in a probate, or a licensed appraiser outside probate.
Value 2 Today's value, as is For: the list price and the family's split. Set by: recent sales near the house, in similar condition.
If a court confirms the sale The confirmation appraisal For: a court confirmed sale, where the price must be at least 90 percent of it. Set by: an appraisal dated within one year before the confirmation hearing.

Do you need your own appraisal if the house goes through probate?

Usually not for the court. In a probate, the court's probate referee appraises the house. A private appraisal does not replace that number. Families sometimes pay for one early, then learn the court still needs the referee's appraisal.

The main home petition works the same way. That is the court process for a primary residence worth up to $750,000. The petition needs an inventory and appraisal by a probate referee, picked by the family from the referees the State Controller appoints for Sacramento County.

The referee's fee is set by law. It is one tenth of one percent of what they appraise, at least $75 and no more than $10,000 unless the court allows more. On a $750,000 house, the commission is $750.

A court can waive the referee for good cause. That takes a petition, a copy of the proposed inventory, and a hearing at least 15 days after filing.

When your own appraisal does make sense: a house held in a living trust. A trust does not go through the court, so there is no referee. The trustee usually hires a licensed appraiser for the date of death value, which sets the tax basis.

Do trust and probate houses sell for less in Folsom?

In this year's sales, the medians per square foot came out about the same. From November 1, 2025 to October 31, 2026, 82 of Folsom's 762 single family sales were marked trust, probate or court confirmation in MetroList. The two groups had similar median prices per square foot: $384 against $382. This group's median days on market was 12, against 17. That counts days listed, not the time it takes to get authority to sell.

The median sale price was lower, $727,475 against $775,000. These houses were also a little smaller and older: a median of 1,990 square feet against 2,097, and built in 1995 against 2000. The group mixes trust sales, probate listings and court confirmation sales, and a trust sale is not always an inheritance. It does not adjust for condition, size or location, so it describes what sold. It does not prove an estate house avoids a discount. A sale counts once if the listing agent checked Trust, Probate Listing or Subject to Court Confirmation, and the rest of Folsom's sales are the comparison. Price the house for the condition it is in today, not for the neighborhood median.

How do I price a house nobody has updated?

Compare two groups of sales near the house: homes that sold in original condition and homes that sold updated. The gap shows what buyers paid for updates. Before I recommend any work, I compare the likely sale proceeds against the contractor bids, the carrying costs the estate pays while the work is done, like the tax bill, insurance and utilities, and the time it adds.
