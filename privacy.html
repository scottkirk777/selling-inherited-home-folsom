What I collect

What you type into the contact form: your name, your phone number or email, the address and anything else you write in. If you call or text, your phone number and message reach me the usual way. The host also keeps standard server logs, described below.

How I use it

To answer you. I do not sell it, and I do not add you to a mailing list. If we end up working together, it goes into my brokerage records like any client file.

Who else handles it

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses. Form messages go through Web3Forms, which emails them to me at scott@wrspg.com. Google signals and ad personalization are turned off, and nothing you type into the form is sent to Google. You can block it with your browser settings or the Google Analytics opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. This site runs no ad trackers. The form also records the page you sent it from and the website or campaign that sent you here, if any, so I know which page helped.

Do Not Track

This site does not track you across other sites, so it treats every visitor the same whether or not a Do Not Track signal is sent.

Email scott@wrspg.com or call 916-847-5822 and I will show you what I have, fix it, or delete it. If we worked on a transaction together, California requires brokers to keep transaction records for three years, so those I keep until then.
